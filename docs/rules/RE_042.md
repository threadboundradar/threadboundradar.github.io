# RE_042 · High Sky Atmosphere cost

| Category | Severity | Fires while | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rendering | Medium | Playing | 30 FPS and 60 FPS |

## What it means

Computing the Sky Atmosphere lookup tables is consuming a significant share of the frame. This is read live from the engine's own stat system, the same as RE_041.

## When it fires

| Measurement | Looks for | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Sky Atmosphere cost | High | GPU time spent computing the Sky Atmosphere lookup tables |

## Why it happens

Sky Atmosphere recomputes its lookup tables based on its configured quality and resolution, every frame by default — cost scales with that quality setting regardless of whether the sky is actually changing.

## How to fix it

1. **Review the configured quality/resolution of the Sky Atmosphere component.**
2. **If the sky is mostly static, consider baking or caching it** instead of recomputing every frame.
3. **Confirm whether Sky Atmosphere is actually needed in this scene**, or a static skybox is enough.

## How to confirm the fix

Run `stat gpu` in the console and watch the **SkyAtmosphereLUTs** row — it is the same stat the plugin reads. Once it falls back under the rule's threshold, the rule stops firing and its row in Diagnostics is no longer marked active.
