# RE_029 · Extreme frame time spike — possible hitch

| Category | Severity | Fires while | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| General | High | Playing | 30 FPS and 60 FPS |

## What it means

A single frame took a very long time to render. This usually indicates a one-off hitch rather than a sustained performance problem.

## When it fires

| Measurement | Looks for | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Frame time | High | Total time to produce this frame |

## Why it happens

Frame times this extreme typically come from a discrete event rather than steady-state rendering or gameplay cost: a streaming load, shader compilation, a Garbage Collection pass, or a mass spawn.

## How to fix it

1. **Check the session history to see if it's an isolated event or a recurring pattern.**
2. **Cross-reference with the CPU/GPU rules active at the same moment** to isolate the cause.

## How to confirm the fix

Watch **Frame Time** in Live Metrics. Once no frame in the window exceeds the rule's threshold, the rule stops firing and its row in Diagnostics is no longer marked active.
