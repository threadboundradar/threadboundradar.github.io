# RE_049 · Landscape without Nanite

| Category | Severity | Fires while | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rendering | Low | Any time — it scans the loaded level | 30 FPS and 60 FPS |

## What it means

The level's landscape has enough components that its triangle count is significant, and Nanite isn't enabled for it.

## When it fires

Both of these are true when the level was last scanned:

| Measurement | Looks for | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Landscape components | High | Landscape components making up the loaded level's terrain |
| Landscape has Nanite | No | Whether Nanite is enabled on the Landscape actor |

## Why it happens

Each Landscape component's triangle count grows with the square of its size, so the total grows fast with the component count. Past a certain size, that adds up to a meaningful triangle budget paid the traditional way instead of through Nanite.

## How to fix it

1. **Enable Nanite on the Landscape** — actor details → Nanite → Enable Nanite.
2. **If Nanite isn't an option, review the Landscape's own LOD settings** in its actor details — how LODs are distributed and at what screen size LOD0 gives way.
3. **Confirm in the level composition table how much of the total the landscape actually contributes.**

## How to confirm the fix

Run **Scan level** again in the Level composition section. Once Nanite is enabled on the Landscape, the rule stops firing and its row in Diagnostics is no longer marked active.
