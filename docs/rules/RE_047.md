# RE_047 · Instancing candidates in the level

| Category | Severity | Fires while | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rendering | Low | Any time — it scans the loaded level | 30 FPS and 60 FPS |

## What it means

There are meshes placed many times in the level as separate components instead of instanced. Each copy pays its own draw call even though they share the same mesh and material.

## When it fires

| Measurement | Looks for | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Instancing candidates | High | Meshes placed enough times as separate components to be worth instancing |

## Why it happens

Placing the same mesh repeatedly by hand — as separate Static Mesh Components or actors — is the most common way a level accumulates draw calls without anyone noticing, since each individual placement looks harmless on its own.

## How to fix it

1. **Convert to an Instanced Static Mesh or Hierarchical ISM.**
2. **Use Merge Actors if they are hand-placed props that are always seen together.**
3. **For vegetation, the Foliage tool already instances by default** — use it instead of placing individual mesh actors.

## How to confirm the fix

Run **Scan level** again in the Level composition section. Once the meshes flagged "instancing candidate" drop to zero, the rule stops firing and its row in Diagnostics is no longer marked active.
