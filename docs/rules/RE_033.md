# RE_033 · Costly Blueprint tick (60 FPS)

| Category | Severity | Fires while | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blueprint | Medium | Playing | 30 FPS and 60 FPS |

## What it means

At least one Blueprint class has a noticeably high Event Tick cost this frame — a significant share of the 60 FPS frame budget on its own.

## When it fires

| Measurement | Looks for | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Blueprint max tick cost | High | The single most expensive Blueprint class's Event Tick cost this frame |

## Why it happens

Blueprint Event Tick graphs that do more work than they need to every frame — loops, repeated casts, expensive function calls — are the usual cause. The cost is per class, so one heavy Blueprint is enough to trigger this even if every other class is cheap.

## How to fix it

1. **Open the Gameplay Profiler panel to identify the flagged class**, then review its Event Tick graph.
2. **Move non-critical tick logic to a Timer with a lower frequency**, or to an event instead of polling every frame.
3. **Consider converting the heaviest logic to C++** if the project allows it.

## How to confirm the fix

Watch the Blueprint tables in the Gameplay Profiler panel. Once the class's tick cost falls back under the rule's threshold, the rule stops firing and its row in Diagnostics is no longer marked active.
