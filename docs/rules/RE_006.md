# RE_006 · Critical GPU-bound (30 FPS)

| Category | Severity | Fires while | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rendering | High | Playing | 30 FPS and 60 FPS |

## What it means

The frame is missing even a 30 FPS budget, and the GPU is sustaining that shortfall. This is the same bottleneck as RE_005, at a level that compromises the lowest reasonable target.

## When it fires

All of these are true at the same time:

| Measurement | Looks for | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| GPU time | High | Time the GPU spends on this frame |
| GPU bound | Yes | The GPU, not the game thread, is what limits the frame |
| Frame time | High | Total time to produce this frame |

## Why it happens

GPU-bound means the GPU, not the CPU, is what sets the frame time. At this severity the specific cause — geometry, shading, fill-rate, or texture bandwidth — is costing enough on its own to put even a 30 FPS target at risk.

## How to fix it

1. **Reduce render resolution or enable TSR upscaling.**
2. **Check which more specific rendering rule is active in the same second** — draw calls, triangle count or shading cost — to find the actual cause.
3. **Consider reducing the quality of costly features (shadows, reflections, post-process) as an immediate mitigation** while you fix the root cause.

## How to confirm the fix

Watch **GPU Time** and **Frame Time** in Live Metrics. Once GPU time falls back under the rule's limit, the rule stops firing and its row in Diagnostics is no longer marked active.
