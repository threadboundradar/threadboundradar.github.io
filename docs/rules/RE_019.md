# RE_019 · GPU time elevated within budget — early signal

| Category | Severity | Fires while | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rendering | Low | Playing | 60 FPS only |

## What it means

The frame still meets its 60 FPS budget, but GPU time already accounts for most of it. If the scene grows even a little more, the GPU will be the first thing to break budget.

## When it fires

All of these are true at the same time:

| Measurement | Looks for | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| GPU time | High | Time the GPU spends on this frame |
| GPU bound | Yes | The GPU, not the game thread, is what limits the frame |
| Frame time | Low | Total time to produce this frame |

This rule only applies to a 60 FPS target. The plugin hides it entirely when the project's target frame rate is set to 30 FPS, where the same measurement can still fit the budget.

## Why it happens

GPU time consuming most of a still-healthy frame budget is the leading indicator for RE_005 — whatever margin is left will absorb only a little more content before the frame crosses over.

## How to fix it

1. **Cross-reference with draw calls and triangle count** to anticipate which GPU-bound rule will trigger first as the scene grows.
2. **Treat this level as a baseline** before adding more content to the scene.

## How to confirm the fix

Watch **GPU Time** relative to **Frame Time** in Live Metrics. Once GPU time falls back under the rule's threshold, the rule stops firing and its row in Diagnostics is no longer marked active.
