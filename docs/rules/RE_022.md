# RE_022 · Extremely high draw calls — multiplatform scalability risk

| Category | Severity | Fires while | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rendering | High | Playing | 30 FPS and 60 FPS |

## What it means

The draw call count is deep into the thousands — risky even if your development PC absorbs it without visible trouble. Platforms with less CPU or RHI bandwidth won't be as forgiving.

## When it fires

| Measurement | Looks for | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Draw calls | High | Commands sent to the GPU this frame |

## Why it happens

This rule doesn't require the frame to be over budget on your current hardware — it flags the count itself as a scalability risk. Older-generation consoles and mobile hardware have far less headroom for draw call submission than a development PC.

## How to fix it

1. **Treat this as technical debt even if the current frame rate is acceptable on your dev PC.**
2. **Establish a draw call budget per target platform** before adding more content that relies on this level of draw call count.

## How to confirm the fix

Watch **Draw Calls** in Live Metrics. Once it falls back under the rule's threshold, the rule stops firing and its row in Diagnostics is no longer marked active.
