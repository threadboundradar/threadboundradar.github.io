# RE_018 · High triangle count — early warning

| Category | Severity | Fires while | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rendering | Low | Playing | 30 FPS and 60 FPS |

## What it means

The scene is already drawing a notably high number of triangles per frame — still far from the critical threshold in RE_002, but worth a look before geometry density keeps growing.

## When it fires

| Measurement | Looks for | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Triangles drawn | High | Triangles submitted this frame, counted once per rendering pass |

## Why it happens

This usually creeps up gradually as new assets get added to a level without anyone checking whether they came in with LODs or with an unnecessarily dense import.

## How to fix it

1. **Check whether recently added assets have LODs configured.**
2. **Evaluate Nanite preventively** on high-density meshes that are still being added to the level.

## How to confirm the fix

Watch **Triangles** in Live Metrics. Once it falls back under the rule's threshold, the rule stops firing and its row in Diagnostics is no longer marked active.
