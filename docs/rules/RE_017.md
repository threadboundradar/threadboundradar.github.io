# RE_017 · High draw calls — early warning

| Category | Severity | Fires while | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rendering | Low | Playing | 30 FPS and 60 FPS |

## What it means

The draw call count is already high even though the frame is still within budget. This is an early signal before reaching the critical threshold in RE_001.

## When it fires

| Measurement | Looks for | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Draw calls | High | Commands sent to the GPU this frame |

## Why it happens

Draw calls tend to climb steadily as a level fills with hand-placed props — fences, rocks, modular pieces — each added as its own actor instead of an instanced copy.

## How to fix it

1. **Identify which meshes repeat most in the level** and evaluate instancing them preventively.
2. **Check whether the growth comes from a specific system** — foliage, props, or particles — rather than the level in general.

## How to confirm the fix

Watch **Draw Calls** in Live Metrics. Once it falls back under the rule's threshold, the rule stops firing and its row in Diagnostics is no longer marked active.
