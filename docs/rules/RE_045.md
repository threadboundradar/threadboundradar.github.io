# RE_045 · Meshes missing LODs in the level

| Category | Severity | Fires while | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rendering | Medium | Any time — it scans the loaded level | 30 FPS and 60 FPS |

## What it means

There are meshes in the level with a single LOD and enough triangles for camera distance to matter. Without LODs, they draw at full density no matter how far away they are, and every rendering pass that includes them pays for the whole mesh.

## When it fires

| Measurement | Looks for | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Meshes missing LODs | High | Meshes in the loaded level with a single LOD and enough triangles for distance to matter |

The per-mesh triangle threshold that decides what counts is set in **Project Settings → Plugins → Threadbound Radar → Level Scan**.

## Why it happens

A mesh with only one LOD is rasterized at the same detail whether it fills the screen or sits at the horizon. On dense meshes, that cost is paid identically in every rendering pass that touches it — base pass, shadow cascades, and any reflection or velocity pass.

## How to fix it

1. **Generate LODs from the mesh's own editor** — Static Mesh Editor → LOD Settings → Number of LODs.
2. **If it's dense static content, consider Nanite instead.** Nanite is the alternative to having LODs, not something needed in addition to them.
3. **Check the level composition table** to see which meshes these are and how much each one contributes.

## How to confirm the fix

Run **Scan level** again in the Level composition section. Once the meshes flagged "no LODs" drop to zero, the rule stops firing and its row in Diagnostics is no longer marked active.
