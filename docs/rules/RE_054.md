# RE_054 · Recurring hitches during play

| Category | Severity | Fires while | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| General | Medium | Playing | 30 FPS and 60 FPS |

## What it means

Frames that take far longer than the ones around them keep happening. A hitch this size disappears inside an average — it's the only thing a player actually notices, even when every other number in the panel looks fine.

## When it fires

| Measurement | Looks for | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Hitches | High | Frames that take far longer than the ones around them |

The Diagnostics row quotes how many hitches it saw and how bad the worst one was.

## Why it happens

Plenty of ordinary frames and one huge spike still average out to a number that reads as "a bit slow" and hides the actual spike completely. Counting hitches instead of averaging catches what the average erases.

## How to fix it

1. **Cross-reference with the CPU vs GPU chart.** If the hitches fall on a shaded segment, it's loading or shader compilation, not gameplay logic.
2. **If they fall during stable play, check the Gameplay Profiler panel.** An occasional costly Blueprint tick is the usual suspect.
3. **A hitch that coincides with a spike in process memory points to a large allocation**, not frame cost.
4. **Garbage Collection passes are the other classic cause of periodic hitches.**

## How to confirm the fix

The Hitches tile in Live Metrics shows the hitches have stopped, and the rule's row in Diagnostics is no longer marked active.
