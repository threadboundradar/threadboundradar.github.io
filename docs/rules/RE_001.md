# RE_001 · Too many draw calls without instancing

| Category | Severity | Fires while | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rendering | High | Playing | 30 FPS and 60 FPS |

## What it means

The render thread is over the rule's limit just preparing draw calls, and there are
thousands of them. The GPU is waiting on that work too. The usual reason is simple: the same mesh is
placed many times as separate actors, and each copy is submitted to the GPU on its own.

## When it fires

All of these are true at the same time:

| Measurement | Looks for | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Render thread time | High | Time the render thread spends building the frame for the GPU |
| Draw calls | High | Commands sent to the GPU this frame |
| GPU bound | Yes | The GPU, not the game thread, is what limits the frame |

## Why it happens

Every separate Static Mesh Component becomes at least one draw call, even when a hundred of them share
the same mesh and material. Instancing lets the engine submit all of those copies as one call. Levels
built by duplicating props by hand — fences, rocks, lamp posts, modular walls — reach thousands of calls
long before the triangle count becomes a problem.

## How to fix it

1. **Convert repeated actors to a Hierarchical Instanced Static Mesh (HISM).** Select the copies in the
   level, then open **Tools → Merge Actors** and use the **Batch** option to replace them with one instanced component.
2. **Enable Nanite on eligible high-poly meshes.** Open the mesh, and under **Nanite Settings** check
   **Enable Nanite Support**. Nanite meshes no longer cost one draw call each.
3. **Find the worst offenders first.** In the level's composition table, look for any mesh placed a
   large number of times — those give the largest drop per change.

## How to confirm the fix

Play again and watch **Draw Calls** and **Render Thread** in Live Metrics. Once draw calls drop back
under the rule's threshold or the render thread returns under its budget, the rule stops firing and its row in
Diagnostics is no longer marked active.
