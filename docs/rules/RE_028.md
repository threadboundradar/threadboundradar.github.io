# RE_028 · Extreme CPU-bound — possible gameplay hitch

| Category | Severity | Fires while | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| CPU | High | Playing | 30 FPS and 60 FPS |

## What it means

The game thread alone took a very long time this frame — a pathological level attributable to the CPU by itself, well beyond simply missing a 30 FPS target.

## When it fires

All of these are true at the same time:

| Measurement | Looks for | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Game thread time | High | Time the main game thread spends this frame |
| GPU bound | No | The GPU is not what limits the frame |

## Why it happens

A spike this large is more often a one-off hitch than a sustained problem: a Garbage Collection pass, a synchronous asset load, or a mass spawn of actors are the classic causes.

## How to fix it

1. **Check for Garbage Collection, synchronous asset loading, or mass spawns** as typical causes of a spike this size.
2. **Profile the specific frame with Unreal Insights** instead of relying on averages, which hide a spike like this.

## How to confirm the fix

Watch **Game Thread** in Live Metrics. Once it stays back under the rule's threshold, the rule stops firing and its row in Diagnostics is no longer marked active.
