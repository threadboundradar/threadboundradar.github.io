# RE_037 · Critical aggregate Blueprint tick cost

| Category | Severity | Fires while | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blueprint | High | Playing | 30 FPS and 60 FPS |

## What it means

The sum of Event Tick cost across all Blueprint classes is at a critical level for a 60 FPS target, consumed by Blueprint tick logic alone.

## When it fires

| Measurement | Looks for | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Blueprint total tick cost | High | Sum of Event Tick cost across all Blueprint classes this frame |

## Why it happens

At this total, Blueprint tick logic isn't a minor contributor to the frame — it's one of the largest single costs on the game thread, whether that cost comes from one class or many.

## How to fix it

1. **Treat this as a CPU optimization priority**, alongside the Game Thread Saturated rules.
2. **Check the Gameplay Profiler panel to identify where to start.**

## How to confirm the fix

Watch the Blueprint tables in the Gameplay Profiler panel for the classes you changed. Once the combined tick cost falls back under the rule's threshold, the rule stops firing and its row in Diagnostics is no longer marked active.
