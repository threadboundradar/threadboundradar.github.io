# RE_012 · Frame outside the critical 30 FPS budget

| Category | Severity | Fires while | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| General | High | Playing | 30 FPS and 60 FPS |

## What it means

The whole frame took longer than even a 30 FPS budget allows. Like RE_011, this rule doesn't diagnose why by itself — check what else fired in the same second.

## When it fires

| Measurement | Looks for | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Frame time | High | Total time to produce this frame |

## Why it happens

A frame can miss budget for many different reasons — this rule only confirms that it happened, at a severity where even players who don't normally notice frame rate will feel it.

## How to fix it

1. **Check the Diagnostics table for other rules active in the same second** — that tells you whether the cause is on the CPU, GPU or memory side.
2. **Treat as a priority.** This level of frame time is noticeable even to players not sensitive to frame rate.

## How to confirm the fix

Watch **Frame Time** in Live Metrics. Once it falls back under the 30 FPS frame budget, the rule stops firing and its row in Diagnostics is no longer marked active.
