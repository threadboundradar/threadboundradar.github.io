# RE_050 · LOD chain that doesn't reduce

| Category | Severity | Fires while | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rendering | Medium | Any time — it scans the loaded level | 30 FPS and 60 FPS |

## What it means

There are meshes with several LODs whose last LOD still keeps most of LOD0's triangles. The chain exists, but it doesn't do anything — the mesh costs almost the same far away as it does close up.

## When it fires

| Measurement | Looks for | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Meshes with weak LOD chains | High | Meshes in the loaded level whose last LOD keeps most of LOD0's triangles |

A healthy LOD chain reaches its last LOD at a small fraction of the original triangle count. The threshold that decides what counts as "weak" is set in **Project Settings → Plugins → Threadbound Radar → Level Scan**.

## Why it happens

LODs are often set up by distance alone, without checking what percentage of triangles each level actually removes. A LOD chain can look complete — several LOD levels, sensible distances — while barely reducing triangle count at all, if the reduction settings behind it were never tuned.

## How to fix it

1. **Review the Reduction Settings of each LOD in the Static Mesh Editor** — the triangle percentage, not just the distance.
2. **If the mesh can't take reduction without visible artifacts, consider Nanite instead.**
3. **Confirm in the level composition table whether that mesh contributes enough to be worth the work.**

## How to confirm the fix

Run **Scan level** again in the Level composition section. Once the meshes flagged "LODs barely reduce" drop to zero, the rule stops firing and its row in Diagnostics is no longer marked active.
