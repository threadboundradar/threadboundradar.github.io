# RE_023 · Extreme triangle count — scalability risk

| Category | Severity | Fires while | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rendering | High | Playing | 30 FPS and 60 FPS |

## What it means

The scene is drawing an extremely high number of triangles per frame — a geometry density that compromises scalability to less powerful hardware, regardless of the current frame rate.

## When it fires

| Measurement | Looks for | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Triangles drawn | High | Triangles submitted this frame, counted once per rendering pass |

## Why it happens

This rule doesn't require the current frame to be over budget — it flags the geometry density itself as a risk. A scene this dense leaves little room to scale down for weaker hardware without a deliberate LOD or Nanite strategy.

## How to fix it

1. **Audit which assets contribute most to this total** using Nanite Visualization or per-mesh triangle counts.
2. **Establish a triangle budget per scene** before the count keeps growing.

## How to confirm the fix

Watch **Triangles** in Live Metrics. Once it falls back under the rule's threshold, the rule stops firing and its row in Diagnostics is no longer marked active.
