# RE_015 · Possible fill-rate or post-process bound

| Category | Severity | Fires while | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rendering | Medium | Playing | 30 FPS and 60 FPS |

## What it means

GPU time is high while both triangle count and draw calls are low — geometry isn't the cost here. Full-screen work like post-processing or the render resolution itself is the likely cause.

## When it fires

All of these are true at the same time:

| Measurement | Looks for | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| GPU time | High | Time the GPU spends on this frame |
| Triangles drawn | Low | Triangles submitted this frame, counted once per rendering pass |
| Draw calls | Low | Commands sent to the GPU this frame |
| GPU bound | Yes | The GPU, not the game thread, is what limits the frame |

## Why it happens

Fill-rate cost scales with how many pixels get shaded, not with how much geometry is on screen. Costly post-process chains, a high render resolution, or full-screen effects like bloom, motion blur, or extensive translucency all pay this cost regardless of scene complexity.

## How to fix it

1. **Review the chain of active Post Process Volumes and their individual cost.**
2. **Reduce render resolution or enable TSR upscaling.**
3. **Check for full-screen translucency overdraw** in particles, 3D UI, or screen effects.

## How to confirm the fix

Watch **GPU Time** in Live Metrics. Once it falls back under the rule's threshold, the rule stops firing and its row in Diagnostics is no longer marked active.
